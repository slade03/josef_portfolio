# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is hiring managers, design leaders, product leaders, and engineering leads evaluating Josef for UI/UX Engineer roles. They usually review the portfolio quickly on a laptop, then return to promising projects for evidence of product thinking, interface craft, technical fluency, and collaboration.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio should make Josef's capabilities easy to understand and easy to trust. It should establish credibility through shipped work, production-ready interface experience, technical execution, and a clear point of view. Success means a reviewer can identify Josef's role, strongest projects, working approach, and contact path without searching through separate pages.

## Brand Personality

Playful, precise, resourceful.

The voice is personable and direct. It can be lively without becoming casual or self-congratulatory. It should describe specific work and decisions instead of relying on broad claims.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated portfolio templates.
- Corporate SaaS layouts built from repetitive cards, muted gradients, and interchangeable copy.
- Editorial-design cliches such as display-serif headlines, tiny monospace labels, drop caps, and rule-heavy magazine grids used without an editorial reason.
- Literal imitation of [Michael Tsirakis's portfolio](https://michaeltsirakis.com/), including its blue identity, basketball portrait concept, wording, exact compositions, or employer-specific storytelling.
- Dark sci-fi interfaces that treat technical ability as a visual costume.

## Design Principles

1. Show real work before making claims.
2. Connect design decisions to implementation details.
3. Make personality useful to the hiring story.
4. Preserve factual integrity, especially roles, dates, project scope, and outcomes.
5. Treat accessibility and performance as evidence of craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic landmarks, logical heading order, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, descriptive link text, sufficient color contrast, and meaningful alternative text. Every motion effect must have a complete reduced-motion behavior that keeps the same content and functionality available.
